# User Guide

## 1. Register an Account

1. Open the **OVALO (FLORYNN)** mobile app.
2. Go to the **More** tab (Profile/Account area).
3. Tap **Create account** (Register).
4. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, **Username**, **Password**, and **Confirm Password**.
5. Read the terms and tap the checkbox to agree.
6. Tap **Enter** to submit registration.
7. After successful registration, tap **Sign in** to go to the login screen and log in.

## 2. Login

1. Open the **Login** screen from the **More** tab (or from the registration success prompt).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.
4. If the account is valid, the app signs you in and returns you to the main app.
5. **Admin and staff accounts are blocked** from using the customer mobile app. If you try to sign in with a staff/admin account, you will be instructed to use the admin portal instead.

## 3. Browse Bouquets (Products)

1. Open the **Shop** tab to view the bouquet catalog.
2. Use the **Search bar** to search by bouquet name.
3. Use **filters** and **categories** (if available) to narrow down results.
4. Each product card shows key details such as **name**, **price**, and **image** (if provided by the server).

## 4. View Bouquet Details

1. Tap a bouquet from the **Shop** list.
2. Review the bouquet’s **description**, **price**, **stock/availability**, and **image**.
3. If available, you may add the bouquet to your cart.

## 5. Add to Cart

1. From the bouquet detail screen, tap **Add to cart** (if the item is in stock).
2. Open the cart using the **cart icon** in the top bar.
3. Review items in your cart, adjust quantities (if supported), or remove items.

## 6. Submit Checkout (Place Order / Inquiry Order)

1. In the **Cart** screen, tap **Checkout**.
2. Fill in your customer details:
   - Full name
   - Contact number
   - Email address
   - Complete address
   - Delivery location
   - City/Province
   - Additional notes (optional)
3. Review the order summary (items and total).
4. Tap **Place Order** / **Submit** to send your order to the FLORYNN team.
5. After successful submission, an order record is created in the backend for staff/admin review and confirmation.

## 7. View Orders / Order Status

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Open your **Orders** list.
3. Each order displays:
   - Order status (e.g., pending/confirmed/rejected depending on the backend)
   - Total amount
   - Date (if provided)
   - Bouquet thumbnails (if available)
4. Tap an order to view **Order Detail**.

## 8. Send a Message (Contact / Inquiry)

1. Open the **Contact** section of the app.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, and **Message**.
3. Tap **Submit** to send your inquiry.
4. A confirmation message appears after successful submission.

## 9. Manage Profile

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. View your account information (based on your saved profile and login session).
3. Your saved customer details may be reused during checkout to reduce re-typing.

## 10. Logout

1. Open the **More** tab.
2. Tap **Log out**.
3. The app clears your session and returns you to a guest state (you can still browse the catalog).
